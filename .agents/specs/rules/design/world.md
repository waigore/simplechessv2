# Authoring rules: world and narrative

Read this before editing `specs/design/world.md`. Do not copy these rules into the spec.

Also read [design.md](design.md).

The setting, lore, and story as they shape play. Describe them in full. This is still game design: no screens and no presentation technique except what is communicated and when.

## Setting

Place and time, tone consistent with the vision, and the traits that make this world distinct.

## World rules

Facts of the world that constrain or explain gameplay. Columns: the rule, its gameplay effect, and a link to the feature.

## Factions and groups

Delete this section when the game has none. Otherwise columns: faction, goals, relationship to the player, and links to member characters.

## Story

Premise, structure (linear, branching, emergent, or none), and player agency: what the player can change, and what is fixed.

Beats: where (a stage or flow), what happens, and the player choice (or "None").

## Narrative delivery

How story reaches the player through play (events, dialogue, environment, discovery). State what is communicated and when. Do not specify screens or presentation.

## Experience intent

What the world and story should make the player feel, and how that supports the player fantasy.
