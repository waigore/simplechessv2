# Risk lexicon

Optional whole-set / domain-collision candidates for [`audit-spec-terminology`](../SKILL.md). These words often mean different things in game design, view, UX, and technical writing. They are **not** required glossary entries. Update when new collisions appear in reviews — see [README.md](README.md).

Do not invent tags beyond the skill's finding table. A lexicon hit with one clear, in-domain sense and no conflicting glossary rows is not a finding.
