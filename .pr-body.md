## Summary

Rework of the `demos/shared-game` cafe-table demo for three of the four playtest polish items in defect #427, plus the README corrections the #63 acceptance run asked for.

- **Pieces were drawn below the spec.** The twelve catalog sprites carry transparent padding, so scaling the full 512 px canvas drew bodies of 0.51–0.72 square units against `SPC.E1`'s 0.85. Each body is now fitted to the extent on one ground line; the artwork's own King → Pawn order (`FRM.P2`) is kept.
- **Coordinates failed contrast.** The old cream `PAL.C10` labels sat on the light margin (1.28:1). They now sit on the board's wooden lip in `PAL.C9` ink at the `UIS.T2` size.
- **No bevel.** A solid wooden bevel (lit `PAL.C2` lips on top/left, deepened `PAL.C5` on bottom/right, `PAL.C5` raised frame, line darker than the fill per `SRF.O1`) now frames the 8×8 inside the board's outer margin, covering no square.

Item 2 (select cue) is **not** in this PR: it is an approved, PO-confirmed style anchor and a fix needs an asset regeneration through `manage-asset`/ElevenLabs. The measured cause is recorded in the evidence folder.
